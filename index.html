About us

B's Chop Bar started as a small table outside Auntie B's house. Students kept coming back, so the table became a kitchen. We still cook everything fresh every morning and we still close when the pot is empty.

Why students like us

  • Big portions at student prices
  • Food is ready in under ten minutes
  • Chop Club members get 10% off orders of GHS 100 or more
